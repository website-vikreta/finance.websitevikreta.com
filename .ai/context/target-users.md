# Target Users — Finance Tool

## Primary: Manager
**Context:**
- Our own company manager. Knows the clients, knows the money, not an accountant.
- Logs transactions between other work, often right after a payment lands or a bill is paid.
- Switches between INR and USD clients daily.
- Uses desktop for review, phone for quick entry.

**Pain they feel:**
- "Which sheet has the latest numbers?"
- "Was this $500 converted at today's rate or last month's?"
- "Did someone already enter this payment?"
- "I can't answer 'are we up this month?' without an hour of work."

**What they need from every screen:**
- Add an entry in seconds (form or one chat sentence).
- See income, expense, net at one glance.
- Trust the number: exact, traceable, no duplicates.

**Target feeling:** CERTAIN. "I know exactly where the money stands."

---

## Secondary: Admin
- Everything a manager does, plus users, roles, categories, audit log.
- Cares about correctness and history: who changed what, when.
- Needs dense, scannable tables more than big visuals.

## Later: Viewer
- Read-only analytics. Design dashboards so they work with every write action hidden.

---

## What All Users Share
- Time-poor. Screens must be scannable: numbers first, labels second.
- Internal tool: no persuasion, no onboarding fluff, no marketing copy.
- Trust is earned by accuracy, not decoration.
